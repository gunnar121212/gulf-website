About

October 2026

Economic impact of a wind farm

ISK 5.5bnin revenue to the municipality over the life of the project
The question
Qair Ísland plans a wind farm of about 200 MW at Hnotasteinn on Hólaheiði. GULF assessed the economic benefits for the local community, including jobs, revenue and infrastructure in Norðurþing and nearby communities.
Result
The wind farm creates about 450 direct person-years of work over its lifetime and brings Norðurþing about ISK 5.5bn in property tax and local income tax. Many indirect and induced jobs would follow on top of that. The project also lays the ground for a stronger grid and better transport in the area, and the added power generation can support new business activity such as data centres and land-based fish farming.
Use
The analysis fed into the project’s environmental impact assessment and its open consultation. GULF presented the findings to residents at open meetings in Kópasker and Raufarhöfn.

May 2026

The public value of Hugarafl

ISK 480ma year in benefit to the public sector
The question
Hugarafl is a grassroots organisation of people with mental health challenges, built on recovery-oriented peer support. GULF assessed what its work is worth to the state and municipalities and compared its funding with similar services.
Result
The share of users in work, study or other activity rises from 15% to 38% and mental wellbeing improves markedly. The public benefit is about ISK 480m a year, mainly through lower benefit payments and higher tax revenue. Yet funding per user is 55–80% lower than at comparable services.
Use
The findings were presented at Hugarafl’s open meetings. Since then the report has supported Hugarafl in its dialogue with government, public bodies and the media, with GULF helping to present it.
