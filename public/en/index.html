October 2026

Economic impact of a wind farm

ISK 5.5bnin revenue to the municipality over the life of the project
The question
Qair Ísland plans a 200 MW wind farm at Hnotasteinn on Hólaheiði. GULF assessed its effects on nearby communities and Norðurþing, including jobs, municipal revenue and infrastructure.
Result
The wind farm creates about 450 direct person-years of work over its lifetime and brings Norðurþing about ISK 5.5bn, mostly in property tax. It also strengthens the local grid and roads and can lay the ground for new activity such as data centres and land-based fish farming.
Use
The analysis fed into the project’s environmental impact assessment and its open consultation. GULF presented the findings to residents at open meetings in Kópasker and Raufarhöfn.

May 2026

The public value of Hugarafl

ISK 482ma year in benefit to the public sector
The question
Hugarafl is a grassroots organisation of people with mental health challenges, built on recovery-oriented peer support. GULF assessed what its work is worth to the state and municipalities and compared its funding with similar services.
Result
The share of users in work, study or other activity rises from 15% to 38% and mental wellbeing improves markedly. The public benefit is about ISK 482m a year, mainly through lower benefit payments and higher tax revenue. Yet funding per user is 55–80% lower than at comparable services.
Use
The findings were presented at Hugarafl’s open meetings and in the media. The report has since been used in Hugarafl’s dialogue with government and public bodies, with support from GULF.
