October 2026

Economic impact of a wind farm

ISK 5.5bnin revenue to the municipality
The question
Qair Ísland plans a wind farm of about 200 MW at Hnotasteinn on Hólaheiði. GULF assessed the economic benefits for the local community, including jobs, revenue and infrastructure in Norðurþing and nearby communities.
Result
The wind farm creates about 450 direct person-years of work over its lifetime and brings Norðurþing about ISK 5.5bn in property tax and local income tax. A further 390 or so indirect and induced person-years of work would follow. The project also lays the ground for a stronger grid and better transport in the area, and the added power generation can support new business activity such as data centres and land-based fish farming.
Use
The analysis fed into the project’s environmental impact assessment and its open consultation. GULF presented the findings to residents at open consultation meetings in Kópasker and Raufarhöfn.

May 2026

The public value of Hugarafl

ISK 480 millionestimated annual benefit to the public sector
The question
Hugarafl is a grassroots organisation of people with mental health challenges, built on a recovery-oriented peer support approach. Its work includes vocational rehabilitation and supporting people to participate more fully in society. GULF assessed the value of its work to the state and municipalities and compared its public funding with that of similar services.
Result
Among Hugarafl’s service users, the share in work, education or other activity rose from 15% to 38%, while mental wellbeing improved markedly. The benefit to the public sector was estimated at around ISK 480 million a year, reflecting factors including increased employment, lower benefit payments and reduced use of mental health services. Public funding per user was also 55–80% lower than for comparable services.
Use
The findings were presented at Hugarafl’s open meetings. The report has since supported the organisation’s discussions with government, public bodies and the media. GULF has also helped Hugarafl present the findings.
